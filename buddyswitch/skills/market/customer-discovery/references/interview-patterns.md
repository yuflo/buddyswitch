# Interview Patterns

## Problem Discovery Prompts
- Ask for the last time the user faced the problem.
- Ask what they did before, during, and after the problem showed up.
- Ask what they tried instead of asking what feature they want.

## Recruiting Signals
- Prioritize people already using a workaround.
- Prefer recent pain over hypothetical future pain.
- Exclude users who cannot describe a current workflow.

## Notes
- Keep the first pass conversational and short.
- Follow evidence gaps rather than forcing the full guide when time is limited.
