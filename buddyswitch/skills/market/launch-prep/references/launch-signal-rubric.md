# Launch Signal Rubric

## Success Signal Qualities
- Specific enough to observe in the first launch window.
- Connected to audience response, not vanity reach alone.
- Useful for deciding whether to continue, adjust, or stop.

## Failure Signal Qualities
- Trigger a clear change in plan.
- Show weak message-channel fit or weak learning quality.
- Avoid depending on hidden analytics the team cannot access.

## Typical Inputs
- Response rate
- Qualified replies
- Waitlist quality
- Follow-up interview acceptance
- Manual conversion checkpoints
