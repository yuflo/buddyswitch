# Interview Prompts

Use these prompts **after** desk research suggests a promising segment.

## Goal

Validate the pain through recent behavior, current workflow, and real tradeoffs.

## Rules

- Ask about the past, not speculation.
- Ask what happened recently.
- Ask what they do today.
- Ask what it costs them.
- Ask what they already tried.
- Avoid asking whether they “would use” your idea.

## Opening prompts

- Walk me through the last time this problem happened.
- What were you trying to get done?
- How do you handle this today?
- What tools, people, or hacks are involved?

## Pain and cost prompts

- What is the most frustrating part of that process?
- How often does this happen?
- What happens when it goes wrong or gets delayed?
- Does it cost time, money, quality, or stress? How?

## Alternative prompts

- What have you tried already?
- Why did you choose that solution?
- What still annoys you about it?
- Have you considered switching? Why or why not?

## Commitment prompts

- Have you ever paid to solve this, directly or indirectly?
- Have you built spreadsheets, docs, or internal workflows around it?
- If someone fixed this well, who on your team would care first?
- What would have to be true for you to try a new solution?

## Anti-patterns to avoid

Do not ask:
- Would you use this?
- Do you like this idea?
- Would you pay for it?
- What features should I build first?

Instead ask for:
- examples
- recent events
- actual spending
- current workarounds
- concrete consequences
