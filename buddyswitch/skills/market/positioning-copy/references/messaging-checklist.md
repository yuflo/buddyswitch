# Messaging Checklist

## Claim Quality
- Can the claim be tied to evidence?
- Is the audience explicit?
- Does the wording avoid invented proof, traction, or certainty?

## Framing Quality
- Does the message make the status quo legible?
- Is the differentiation concrete instead of generic?
- Can a founder say it out loud without extra explanation?

## Common Failure Modes
- Listing features instead of value.
- Sounding broader than the evidence supports.
- Mixing visual design direction into copy recommendations.
