# Secondary Market Investor Analysis

Frame the investable claim before collecting evidence. Distinguish the total
budget pool from the portion the company can capture, and distinguish qualified
players from adjacent names that do not share the same economics.

Test the thesis against evidence quality, competitive durability, value-capture
mechanics, valuation sensitivity, downside cases, and explicit entry conditions.
Record disconfirming evidence and unresolved risk. Do not offer or automate trade
execution, account actions, or short-horizon news gambling.
